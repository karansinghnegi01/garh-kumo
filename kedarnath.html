🛕 About Kedarnath

Kedarnath is one of the most sacred pilgrimage sites in India, located in the majestic Himalayas of Uttarakhand. Surrounded by snow-covered peaks and serene landscapes, it is dedicated to Lord Shiva and forms an important part of the Char Dham Yatra.
